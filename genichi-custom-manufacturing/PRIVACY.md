# Privacy policy: Genichi Custom Manufacturing plugin and Genichi Tool RFQ connector

Last updated: 2026-09-27

This policy covers the Genichi Custom Manufacturing plugin for Claude and the Genichi Tool RFQ connector (the `genichi-rfq` MCP server), operated by Genichi Tool Co., Ltd. (元壹工具有限公司), Daya District, Taichung, Taiwan. Contact: service@genichi-tool.com.

## What we collect

We receive data only when you approve sending a request for quotation (RFQ) and Claude calls the `submit_rfq` tool. We then receive exactly the fields in that request:

- your name and the email address you want us to reply to
- optionally, your company, phone number and country
- the product details you provided: product name, description, quantity, material, dimensions and tolerances, finish, target price, needed-by date, notes and any links you chose to share

The skill in this plugin runs inside Claude and sends nothing to us on its own. We do not receive your conversation, Claude's memory, chat history or files you upload to Claude.

## How it is transmitted and stored

The request travels over HTTPS to our MCP server, which emails it to service@genichi-tool.com. The server does not store requests. It logs only the reference number, the time and whether delivery succeeded. Our hosting provider's routing logs may record IP addresses for a short period for security and operations.

The email is kept in our business mailbox as correspondence about your inquiry.

## How we use it

We use the request to evaluate it, answer your questions, prepare a quote, and handle any order that follows. We do not sell your data, and we do not add you to marketing lists without your consent.

## How long we keep it

We keep inquiry emails for as long as needed to handle the inquiry and any resulting order, and as required by law. You can ask us to delete your request at any time.

## Your choices and rights

You decide whether a request is sent; nothing is sent without your approval. To access, correct or delete your data, email service@genichi-tool.com. We handle personal data in line with Taiwan's Personal Data Protection Act.

## Changes

We will update this page when these practices change and revise the date above.
