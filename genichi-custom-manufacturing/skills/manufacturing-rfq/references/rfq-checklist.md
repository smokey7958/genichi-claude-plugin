# RFQ checklist by product type

Ask only what the user hasn't already answered. Every request needs the common items; then use the section that matches the product.

## Contents
- Common to every request
- Machined parts (CNC turning and milling)
- Hand tools (forged, stamped or machined)
- Sheet metal parts
- Castings
- Plastic parts
- Kits, sets and private label
- Engraving and customizing existing products
- Prototypes and small batches

## Common to every request

- What the item is, what it does, where it is used
- Quantity per order; yearly volume; one-off or repeat
- Critical dimensions and which features are functional versus cosmetic
- Material, or the requirements that decide it
- Finish, color, marking or logo
- Packaging: bulk, individual bag, blister, box, retail card, barcode
- Target unit price and currency
- Needed-by date; destination country; preferred shipping terms (for example EXW, FOB Taichung, DDP)
- Required standards or certificates (RoHS, REACH, ISO or DIN standard numbers, material certificates, inspection reports)
- Drawings, CAD (STEP or IGES preferred for 3D, DXF or PDF for 2D), photos or a physical sample

## Machined parts (CNC turning and milling)

- Material grade (for example 6061-T6, SUS304, S45C, brass C3604, POM)
- Tolerances on functional features; general tolerance class for the rest (ISO 2768-m is a common default)
- Threads: size, pitch, class, depth
- Surface roughness where it matters (Ra)
- Heat treatment or hardness (HRC), and whether it is before or after machining
- Finish: anodize (type and color), plating (zinc, nickel, chrome), black oxide, passivation, powder coat
- Deburring and edge-break requirements
- Inspection: first-article report, CMM, dimension report per lot

## Hand tools (forged, stamped or machined)

- Tool type and closest existing example (brand and model helps)
- Size or length, jaw or tip shape, capacity (wire gauge, cutting diameter, torque)
- Material and hardness (for example chrome vanadium, S2, carbon tool steel; HRC at cutting edge or tip)
- Standards to meet (ISO, DIN, JIS, ANSI) or required tests (cutting test, torque test, insulation to IEC 60900)
- Handle: dipped, dual-material grip, color, logo on handle
- Finish: black oxide, nickel or chrome plating, polished, satin
- Marking: laser or stamped logo, size marking
- Packaging and private-label artwork

## Sheet metal parts

- Material and thickness
- Flat size, bends (count, angles, inside radius), holes and cut-outs
- Hardware inserts (PEM nuts, studs), welding
- Finish: powder coat color and gloss, plating, brushed, raw
- Flatness and cosmetic faces

## Castings

- Casting method if known (sand, investment or lost-wax, die casting)
- Alloy (for example A380 aluminium, ductile iron, SUS304)
- Part weight and envelope size
- Machined features after casting and their tolerances
- Surface quality, porosity or pressure-test requirements
- Tooling ownership and expected tool life

## Plastic parts

- Resin (ABS, PP, PA66-GF30, POM, PC) and color (RAL or Pantone)
- Wall thickness, overall size, expected yearly volume (decides mold type)
- Texture or gloss, logo in the mold
- Overmolding or inserts
- Mold ownership, cavity count, expected mold life

## Kits, sets and private label

- List of items with specs and quantities per set
- Case or tray: blow-molded, EVA foam, fabric roll, metal box
- Artwork: logo files, packaging design, labeling language, barcodes
- Retail requirements: hang tags, warnings, country-of-origin marking

## Engraving and customizing existing products

- The product (supplied by the customer or sourced by the factory)
- Mark type: laser engraving, color marking, printing
- Content and file (vector AI, SVG or DXF preferred), size and position
- Material of the surface (metal, wood, plastic, acrylic, glass)
- Quantity and whether each piece is different (serial numbers, names)

## Prototypes and small batches

- Number of prototypes and whether they must match the production process
- Which features must be validated (fit, function, look)
- Budget and timeline for prototypes versus production
- Planned production volume after approval
