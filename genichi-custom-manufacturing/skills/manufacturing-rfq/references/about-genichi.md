## What Genichi Tool makes

- Hand tools: pliers, diagonal cutters, nippers, wire strippers, screwdrivers, socket sets, tweezers, measuring tapes, soldering and marking tools
- Tool sets and kits, and tool storage
- Laser engraving and marking on metal, wood, plastic and acrylic

## Custom and OEM/ODM work

Genichi Tool takes on projects beyond its catalog: custom tools, private-label versions of existing tools, parts made to a customer's drawing, and development from a sample or an idea. The team reviews every request, including unusual ones, and replies with feasibility, suggested process options and a quote. Genichi Tool coordinates the whole process chain within central Taiwan's tool-making cluster: forging, machining, heat treatment, surface finishing, assembly, marking and packaging.

## How a request is handled

1. The request arrives at service@genichi-tool.com.
2. Genichi Tool reviews it and replies to the customer's email, with questions if anything is unclear.
3. After the quote is accepted: drawing confirmation, tooling if needed, samples for approval, then production and inspection.

Customers can send drawings, CAD files or photos by replying to Genichi Tool's email, or to service@genichi-tool.com with the RFQ reference number in the subject.
